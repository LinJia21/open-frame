## 4. Narrative analysis (six dimensions)

Company-sourced coverage consistently uses 'human-led', 'human-centric' and 'remove friction, not replace judgment' language and quotes only executives. Labour-side material frames AI as displacement, relying on executive expectations and forecasts rather than observed outcomes. The two data-driven pieces complicate both: postings show real but minority AI hiring; headcount data show no decimation yet. No article quotes a working animator.

- **Main Argument**: Arguments range from 'AI is quietly being adopted' and 'AI is a human-led tool' to 'AI will displace most animation labour' and 'displacement has not happened yet'.
- **Causal Attribution**: Job changes are attributed to AI in labour-side and festival coverage, but mainly to business factors (tastes, strategy, tax incentives) in the data column; cost savings are attributed to AI platforms by companies.
- **Responsibility Attribution**: Studios (concealing adoption), commentators (overstating displacement) and legislators (controlling impact) are each assigned responsibility by different articles.
- **Language Framing**: 'Human-led/human-centric' and 'inflection point' (companies) vs. 'worst fears' and 'disruption' (labour-side) vs. 'Not yet' (data).
- **Source Selection**: Executives dominate; labour appears through a commissioned survey and a festival director; one anonymous source; no working animators.
- **Underrepresented Perspectives**: Working animators, VFX artists and voice actors; audiences; independent cost data; non-US markets.

Key patterns:
- Adoption is reported via primary announcements; displacement via forecasts and perceptions.
- Three announcement-based articles share one original source.
- Workers' own voices are absent from the analysed texts.
- Different metrics (postings, headcount, executive perceptions) are often conflated in public debate.

These observations describe individual articles only. With 1–3 articles per outlet, no claims are made about any outlet as a whole, and no political labels are applied.
